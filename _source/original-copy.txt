Internal Family Systems (IFS)
Heal the parts of you still living in the past
In plain terms
we get to know the inner critic and the people-pleaser instead of fighting them.
IFS helps you understand the different parts of yourself — the inner critic, the people-pleaser, the anxious protector. We'll compassionately work with these parts to unburden them from old pain, so you can reconnect with your authentic Self and stop operating from survival mode.

Meet Elyce
Hi, I'm Elyce — The Feeling Expert
I'm a Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor, a Certified Internal Family Systems Therapist, and an International Integral Sound Healing Practitioner. I specialize in helping high-achieving women heal from complex trauma, regulate their nervous systems, and reconnect with their authentic selves.
My approach goes beyond traditional talk therapy. I combine evidence-based methods with holistic healing practices, because true transformation happens when we address trauma at every level — mental, emotional, physical, and spiritual.
Why “The Feeling Expert”? Elyce to confirm
Because the women I work with are usually experts at everything except feeling. They can analyse, manage, perform and hold it all together. What they've lost is the ability to feel safe in their own body. That's the part I help you get back.
