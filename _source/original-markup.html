How I help

My three-pillar approach to trauma healing

Three things have to happen for trauma to actually lift. We work on all three.

  1. Nervous system regulation
  2. Internal Family Systems
  3. Holistic trauma healing
1 Nervous system regulation

Restore safety in your body

In plain termswe teach your body that the danger has passed.

Your nervous system holds the memory of your trauma. Through polyvagal-informed therapy and somatic experiencing, we'll help your body finally recognize that the threat is over. You'll learn to regulate your emotions, calm your stress response, and feel grounded in your own skin.

2 Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Heal the parts of you still living in the past

In plain termswe get to know the inner critic and the people-pleaser instead of fighting them.

IFS helps you understand the different parts of yourself — the inner critic, the people-pleaser, the anxious protector. We'll compassionately work with these parts to unburden them from old pain, so you can reconnect with your authentic Self and stop operating from survival mode.

The parts, and the Self underneath

Inner critic People-pleaser Self Anxious protector The one holding it together

Every part is trying to protect you. None of them is the enemy.

3 Holistic trauma healing

Integrate mind, body, and spirit

In plain termswe use more than talking — sound, breath and movement help release what's stored.

True healing happens when we address the whole person. In addition to evidence-based therapy, I integrate sound healing, breathwork, mindfulness, and somatic practices to help release trauma stored in your body and reconnect you with a sense of peace and wholeness.

Meet Elyce

Hi, I'm Elyce — The Feeling Expert

I'm a Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor, a Certified Internal Family Systems Therapist, and an International Integral Sound Healing Practitioner. I specialize in helping high-achieving women heal from complex trauma, regulate their nervous systems, and reconnect with their authentic selves.

My approach goes beyond traditional talk therapy. I combine evidence-based methods with holistic healing practices, because true transformation happens when we address trauma at every level — mental, emotional, physical, and spiritual.

Why “The Feeling Expert”?Elyce to confirm

Because the women I work with are usually experts at everything except feeling. They can analyse, manage, perform and hold it all together. What they've lost is the ability to feel safe in their own body. That's the part I help you get back.
